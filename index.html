How I prepare a home for market

Sellers often tell me the hardest part is not knowing whether they're getting it right. So I make every decision visible.

  1. Pricing you can see

    Before we choose a number, I walk you through the evidence: recent sales, price per square foot, how your street has moved over time and what buyers are touring right now. You'll know exactly why the price is the price.

  2. A staging consultation, included

    A professional stager walks through your home with us before photos, so every room shows at its best. Small changes, clearly prioritized, with no pressure to renovate.

  3. A launch built around your buyer

    Professional photography, video and a 3D tour, a coming soon campaign, targeted digital ads, and direct outreach to agents who are working with active buyers.

  4. Negotiation, handled

    As a Certified Negotiation Expert, I protect your price and your terms through offers, inspection, appraisal and closing, and I tell you plainly what each decision means for your bottom line.
